03

Why another cleaner?

A cleanup app should not need trust gymnastics.

Storage Clean is for the moment your disk is almost full and every click feels dangerous. It shows what is taking space, why it is probably safe or unsafe, and what will happen before anything is removed.

The goal is simple: make the right action obvious, keep your files local, and never turn cleanup into a subscription funnel.
